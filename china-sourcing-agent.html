China Sourcing Agent for Importers in Mexico and Latin America

SourciaVera is a China sourcing agent, with a team in Guangzhou, for importers in Mexico and Latin America: we find the factory, negotiate, inspect every order on video and ship. We charge 10% of the order only when the purchase closes and take no factory commissions.

Process

How we work: from search to shipping

You can start with the $49 remote supplier check, which returns a written report within 48 hours, before committing more money. After that, the work follows six steps.

1. Find and verify the factory

We look for factories for your product and confirm they are real. If you already have one, we start with the $49 remote check or an on-site factory verification.

2. Negotiate

We negotiate in Chinese, directly with the factory and on your behalf.

3. Sample

We order and review the sample before moving to bulk production.

4. Production follow-up

Our team in Guangzhou follows production at the factory.

5. Inspection before export

We inspect the order at the factory and record video before it leaves China.

6. Loading and shipping

We coordinate shipping from the factory to your door and give you an all-in price before production (DDP available, duties and delivery included). Customs clearance in Mexico is handled by a licensed customs broker (agente aduanal). If you have a Mexican importer registration (padrón de importadores), the goods are imported under your name so you can credit the VAT. We declare the real invoice value — no undervaluation.

Pricing

What it costs: 10% only when the purchase closes

The sourcing agent commission is 10% of the order value and is charged only when the purchase closes.

On-site verification, inspection and audit are priced separately at our published rates: factory verification from $449 per visit, pre-shipment inspection $399 per man-day and factory audit $499 per man-day. After a detailed consultation, those three services get 20% off. If you first want to see a factory live, a live video factory verification costs $120 per hour.

Quality

Inspection at the factory before export: every order

Every order is inspected at the factory before it is exported, large or small. On small batches (custom orders start at 50 pieces) every piece is checked; on large orders we apply the sampling agreed in advance. Everything is recorded on video and sent to you.

The sampling rules are in our inspection standards, and the kind of evidence you receive is in the sample report.

Warehouse

Our own warehouse in Guangzhou

We do the storage ourselves; it is not outsourced to a partner. In Guangzhou we handle four functions: storage, consolidation, packing and quality checks.

Products

Products: auto parts first, in three categories

We work in three categories, with auto parts first:

  • Auto parts, including OEM/ODM private-label development (see OEM vs ODM for auto parts).
  • Construction.
  • Custom badges, medals and keychains.
Team

A team of about 30 people

We are a team of about 30 people in Guangzhou, across purchasing, transport and logistics, and foreign trade.

Impartiality

We only charge you

We charge the client only: we do not accept commissions from factories, so we have no interest in which factory you choose. The details are in our impartiality statement.
