Choosing a Provider

How to choose a China verification
or inspection provider

Three types of provider compared on language, price transparency, factory rebates, video, follow-up and small orders, with the questions to ask any of them.

Home›How to choose

The short answer

There is no single best provider. The right choice depends on what you need: accredited reports and lab tests, a local team that works in Chinese, or someone to run the whole purchase. Compare providers on how they are paid, whether you get the unedited video, how clear the price is and whether they work with small orders. This guide compares three types of provider. It names no company.

Who each one suits

The buyer each type is best for

Large international inspection companies

Best for: buyers who need accredited reports, laboratory testing, product certification or audits against recognised schemes, or whose own customer requires a named global company. Also a good fit for large, regular programs across many factories.

Local Chinese inspection companies

Best for: buyers with regular volume in one region who have a Chinese-speaking team or contact, and who can manage the relationship themselves.

Sourcing agents and individual middlemen

Best for: buyers who want someone to run the whole purchase, from finding the factory to shipping, and who accept that the person advising them may also earn from the order. Ask how they are paid before you rely on their advice.

Questions to ask

Eight questions for any provider, including us

  1. How are you paid, and by whom?
  2. Do you take anything from the factory, in any form?
  3. Is the price the same whether the factory passes or fails?
  4. Will I receive the unedited video, or only photos?
  5. Who goes to the factory: your employee or a freelancer?
  6. In which language is the report, and who answers my questions?
  7. What is not covered, such as laboratory tests or certificates?
  8. Can I see a sample report before I pay? (Ours: sample report.)
FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What are the main types of China verification and inspection providers?

Three are common: large international inspection companies, local Chinese inspection companies, and sourcing agents or individual middlemen. They differ in language support, price transparency, how they are paid, whether you get video, ongoing follow-up and fit for small orders.

When is a large international inspection company the better choice?

When you need accredited reports, laboratory testing, product certification or an audit against a recognised scheme, or when your own customer requires a named global company.

What is the most important question to ask any provider?

How are you paid, and by whom? If the provider earns anything from the factory, or earns more when your order closes, ask how that is kept apart from the advice you receive.

Should I ask for video, not just photos?

Yes. Photos in a report can be selected and reused. Ask whether you receive the unedited video recorded during the visit.

Where is SourciaVera not the right choice?

If you need laboratory tests, international certificates or an accredited inspection report, a large inspection company or laboratory is a better fit.
