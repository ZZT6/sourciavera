Process · Step 1

Factory selection

What we do: We look for factories for your product and confirm they are real. If you already have one, we can start with the remote check.

What you receive: A written report of the remote check within 48 hours.

Price or term: Remote supplier check: $49.

Process · Step 2

On-site verification

What we do: We go to the factory with a camera and record the visit unedited.

What you receive: The verification report within 48 hours, with the unedited video. We appear in the video.

Price or term: From $449 per visit; 20% off after a detailed consultation.

Process · Step 3

Negotiation and sample

What we do: We negotiate in Chinese, directly with the factory and on your behalf; we order and review the sample before bulk production.

What you receive: Price and terms in writing, and the reviewed sample.

Price or term: A 10% purchasing commission on the order, only when the purchase closes.

Process · Step 4

Production supervision

What we do: Our team in Guangzhou follows production with visits to the factory.

What you receive: A report after each agreed visit, in Spanish or English.

Price or term: Pre-shipment inspection $399 per man-day; factory audit $499 per man-day.

Process · Step 5

Inspection before export

What we do: We inspect every order at the factory before it is exported: on small batches, piece by piece; on large orders, with the agreed sampling. Everything is recorded on video.

What you receive: The video of the inspection before shipment.

Price or term: Quoted at the published rate: $399 per man-day.

Process · Step 6

Shipping and delivery to your door

What we do: We coordinate shipping from the factory to your door and give you an all-in price before production (DDP available, duties and delivery included). Customs clearance in Mexico is handled by a licensed customs broker (agente aduanal). If you have a Mexican importer registration (padrón de importadores), the goods are imported under your name so you can credit the VAT. We declare the real invoice value — no undervaluation.

What you receive: An all-in price agreed before production; DDP available, duties and delivery included.

Price or term: All-in price agreed before production.
