## H2O2 Alibaba Supplier Verification

### Confirmed Facts
Customer region: Not disclosed — source describes only "a client"
Product: 50% hydrogen peroxide
Problem: Client had four Alibaba suppliers of 50% hydrogen peroxide, all carrying the "Verified Supplier" badge and each claiming in-house manufacturing. Based on the badge and product photos alone, the client had been preparing a 30% deposit to one of them
SourciaVera involvement: Checked each of the four suppliers' business licenses against China's national enterprise credit system
Finding: None of the four had a manufacturing scope for hydrogen peroxide — all four were registered as trading or import-export companies, reselling from the same handful of real producers
Result: Not stated in the source. The published post's conclusion is about what an Alibaba "Verified Supplier" badge does and doesn't confirm (it confirms a business exists and passed a basic check; it does not confirm the business manufactures what it sells) — it does not say what the client ultimately did next (proceeded with one of the four as a known trading company, sourced the actual manufacturer, or walked away)
