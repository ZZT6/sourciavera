## BYD Seagull Wheels

### Confirmed Facts
Customer region: Latin America (client described as "one of our Latin American clients")
Product: BYD Seagull spare wheel kit — 15×3.5 steel wheel, 125/80R15 tire, offset ET 30, center bore (CB) 54.1 mm, PCD 4×100, plus jack, lug wrench and storage bag
Problem: Client needed the spare wheel's fitment (offset, center bore, bolt pattern) confirmed correct before paying — a spare wheel has to seat properly without a spacer
SourciaVera involvement: Contacted and evaluated 4 wheel factories on the client's behalf; refused to accept the winning factory's first spec sheet as final and required a formal, stamped specification document; required an actual video of the wheel installed on the vehicle showing it seated without a spacer; filmed size, PCD, offset and hub bore with a real fitment test (video exists, in Spanish, posted to Facebook)
Finding: The winning factory's first spec sheet listed the offset as ET 54.1, which was actually the center bore value (54.1 mm), not the offset — corrected offset is ET 30, a 24 mm discrepancy. Separately, one of the four factories contacted (rejected from the shortlist) had a business license and Alibaba listing giving an address in Chengdu's Jinniu District, but its actual production line was in Hongguang, across the city — registered/listing address did not match the production site
Result: Sample kit ordered through Alibaba Trade Assurance with the corrected, stamped spec. Sample shipped and reached the client in September (year not stated in the source text). Client tested it against the vehicle and confirmed it met the requirement. Conversation has moved to a follow-up bulk order — the bulk order itself has not been confirmed as placed or completed
