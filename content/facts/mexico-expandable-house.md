## Mexico Expandable House Case

### Confirmed Facts
Customer region: Mexico
Customer name: Carlos Mendoza — named, on the record, with photo, in a testimonial already published on the homepage (/index.html, testimonials section)
Product: Expandable (folding) container home
Problem: Client had doubts about a supplier quoting noticeably lower than competitors — specifically, whether the lower price meant a quality shortfall he couldn't see from a listing
SourciaVera involvement: Shared live video and photos via WhatsApp, sent directly from the factory floor in China, assessing the steel profile and the finishes on the folding houses
Finding: Per the client's own words, the videos and photos let him "clearly see the quality of the steel profile and the finishes on the folding houses" — the evidence resolved the doubt about the lower-priced supplier
Result: Client closed the purchase from Mexico. Per the testimonial, the relationship continued: "We'll keep working together on the next container!"
