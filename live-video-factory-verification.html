Live Video Factory Verification in China

A SourciaVera team member walks a factory in China on live HD video while you watch and direct the call, in English and Spanish. It costs $120 per hour, billed by the hour with a one-hour minimum; the recording follows within 24 hours. No written report is included.

How to book and run the call

You book in advance. The steps are the same for any factory in the Guangzhou area:

  1. Tell us the factory and what you want to see. Send the factory name, its address and the machines, areas or documents you care about through the contact form or WhatsApp.
  2. We agree a date and time with you. The call is held on WhatsApp video by default, in English and Spanish. If you prefer, we can use Zoom, Google Meet or Teams instead.
  3. A team member goes to the factory. Guangzhou and the surrounding area are included in the price; for other regions we quote the travel costs separately before you confirm.
  4. You direct the call live. Ask us to move to another machine, open the warehouse area or show a certificate, and we do it while you watch.
  5. We send you the recording. The recording reaches you within 24 hours after the call ends. A written report is not part of this service.

Price and billing

The price is $120 per hour, billed by the hour with a one-hour minimum. Each additional hour is billed at the same $120 rate. The 20% discount that applies to our on-site services after a detailed consultation does not apply to this service.

What you can see during the call

You choose what to look at while the call is running. Typical requests:

  • The production line or the machine that makes your product, as far as the camera can reach.
  • The warehouse and finished-goods area, including pallets, labels and packaging.
  • Certificates and the business license, shown to the camera so you can read them.
  • Your sample or product, close up, from the angles you ask for.

The factory decides which areas we may enter. If something cannot be shown, we tell you during the call so you can decide what to do next.

What a video call cannot confirm

A live video call shows only what is inside the camera frame, at the moment of the call. It does not replace a sampling inspection of your goods or a written audit, and it cannot show what happens outside the frame or on other days.

A document held up to the camera can be read, but it cannot be checked against official records on a call; the $49 remote supplier check looks at company registration records instead.

If you need a written report, sampled inspection or an audit, choose the on-site factory verification (from $449 per visit, with a written report and unedited video) or a pre-shipment inspection ($399 per man-day, described on the factory supervision page).
