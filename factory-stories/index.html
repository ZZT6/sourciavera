How a factory story is built

Same structure, every time

  1. Buyer background — who needed the product, and why they were unsure.
  2. Supplier situation — what the supplier claimed, in their own words.
  3. What we checked — location, production line, equipment, product quality, capacity, communication.
  1. Findings — what buyers in that situation should pay attention to.
  2. Recommendation — what type of buyer this supplier may fit.
  3. Verify your own supplier — a direct path to the same check.
