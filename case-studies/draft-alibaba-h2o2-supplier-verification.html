Customer challenge

A client came to our team with four Alibaba suppliers of 50% hydrogen peroxide. All four carried the "Verified Supplier" badge, and each claimed in-house manufacturing. Based on the badge and product photos alone, the client had been preparing a 30% deposit to one of them.

Supplier situation

Each of the four suppliers presented as a manufacturer on their Alibaba storefront, with the "Verified Supplier" badge as the main signal of legitimacy the client had to go on.

What SourciaVera checked

  • Business license scope — each business license checked against China's national enterprise credit system (gsxt.gov.cn or equivalent).

Finding

None of the four had a manufacturing scope for hydrogen peroxide — all four were registered as trading or import-export companies, reselling from the same handful of real producers.

The badge did what it does: confirm that a business exists and passed a basic check. It does not confirm that the business makes what it sells.
