How we assess a supplier

Three layers — and only the last one is proof.

Most online "supplier checks" stop at layer one. Based on our factory visits in China, the gap between a listing and the real production floor is where buyers lose money.

LAYER 01

Public records

What the company is legally allowed to do, and where it says it operates.

  • Business license & registered scope
  • Registered capital & company age
  • Registered address vs. claimed factory
LAYER 02

Desk analysis

Signals that separate a manufacturer from a trading company, and a fair quote from bait.

  • Trading-company clues in the listing
  • Price sanity vs. material cost
  • Product range too wide for one factory
LAYER 03

Human verification

Someone from our team physically at the factory, filming what exists.

  • Production line & equipment on video
  • Workers and capacity observed
  • Your product checked against spec

FAQ

Checking a Chinese supplier

How can I tell if a Chinese supplier is reliable?

Check three layers: public records (business license, registered scope, registered vs. production address), desk signals (trading-company clues, price sanity, listing history), and on-site evidence (production line, equipment, workers and product checks filmed by someone physically at the factory). Online signals alone cannot prove a factory exists or can make your specification.

Why is my supplier not in the database?

We only list suppliers we have researched ourselves or visited in person, so the database is deliberately small. Send us the link and we will tell you what we can check.

What does the verification status mean?

Verified on-site means our team visited the factory and documented it. Desk-checked means we reviewed public records and online signals but have not visited. Not verified means we have not assessed the supplier yet.

Do suppliers pay to be listed?

No. We take zero commission from factories, and suppliers cannot pay to be listed or to change their status. Listings are buyer guidance, not rankings or endorsements.
