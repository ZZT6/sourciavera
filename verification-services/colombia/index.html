What is different when you import to Colombia

Words that change from country to country

In Colombia, cars are carros, parts are repuestos, and taillights are often called stop. Wheels are rines and tires are llantas, as in Mexico. Spell out the English name next to the local term so the supplier cannot guess.

Time zone and how we communicate

Colombia is on UTC−5 all year, with no summer time, so Guangzhou (UTC+8) is 13 hours ahead. A visit that starts at 9:00 in Guangzhou starts at 20:00 the evening before in Bogotá. Reports come in Spanish or English, and during a visit we can share live video over WhatsApp.

What buyers in Colombia import

Auto parts is our main category, and the guides below apply to buyers in Colombia: auto parts, with construction products such as expandable and container homes and promotional items such as badges, medals and keychains also covered.

Real cases

Ports and customs

Cargo commonly arrives through Cartagena on the Caribbean coast or Buenaventura on the Pacific. Your freight forwarder will quote the route. Import rules, duties and taxes differ by country and change over time. Confirm them with your customs broker (agencia de aduanas (customs broker)). SourciaVera is not a customs broker.
