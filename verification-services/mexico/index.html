What is different when you import to Mexico

Words that change from country to country

In Mexico, rines are wheels and llantas are tires, and balatas are brake pads. Elsewhere in the region the same words can mean something else, so write the English name and the local term on every specification you send.

Time zone and how we communicate

Mexico City is on UTC−6 all year, with no summer time, so Guangzhou (UTC+8) is 14 hours ahead. A visit that starts at 9:00 in Guangzhou starts at 19:00 the evening before in Mexico City. Reports come in Spanish or English, and during a visit we can share live video over WhatsApp.

Ports and customs

Sea freight from China commonly arrives at Pacific ports such as Manzanillo and Lázaro Cárdenas. Your freight forwarder will quote the route. Import rules, duties and taxes differ by country and change over time. Confirm them with your customs broker (agente aduanal (customs broker)). SourciaVera is not a customs broker.
