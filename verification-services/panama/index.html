What is different when you import to Panama

Words that change from country to country

In Panama, parts are repuestos, wheels are rines, tires are llantas, and the trunk is the maletero. The customs broker is a corredor de aduanas, not an agente aduanal as in Mexico. Put the English name next to the local term on every specification.

Time zone and how we communicate

Panama is on UTC−5 all year, with no summer time, so Guangzhou (UTC+8) is 13 hours ahead. A visit that starts at 9:00 in Guangzhou starts at 20:00 the evening before in Panama City. You receive the report in Spanish or English, and we can show you the factory live on WhatsApp during the visit.

About importing from China

Importing from China into Panama: ANA, corredores de aduana and ITBMS

No trade agreement with China

Panama's trade ministry (MICI) publishes the agreements Panama has in force. Its list of bilateral agreements includes free trade agreements with Singapore, Chile, Peru, Canada, Mexico and Israel, a trade promotion agreement with the United States and partial-scope agreements with Cuba, Colombia, the Dominican Republic and Trinidad and Tobago. It names no agreement with China. The general rate of ITBMS, Panama's value-added tax, is 7% (DGI, Law 8 of 2010, article 76).

The Autoridad Nacional de Aduanas and the corredor de aduanas

Panama's customs authority, the Autoridad Nacional de Aduanas (ANA), was created by Decreto Ley 1 of 13 February 2008. Article 42 of that decree requires the intervention of an agente corredor de aduana for the customs regimes, including temporary and definitive imports, unless an international agreement or the law excludes it. Article 43 lists the exceptions, among them imports of personal and household goods by individuals whose CIF value does not exceed B/.4,000 and is not split into smaller lots (Gaceta Oficial). The decree dates from 2008 and later changes may apply, so confirm the current rule with your corredor. A commercial order from a Chinese factory is not personal or household use, so plan for a corredor from the start, and give the corredor a complete set of documents (invoice, packing list, transport document) before the cargo arrives.

Pacific or Atlantic side

For the sea leg, iContainers estimates about 30 to 35 days from Shanghai to Balboa on a Pacific loop service and gives no Shanghai-specific figure for the Atlantic-side terminals (iContainers). Which side suits you depends on where your consignee is, so ask your forwarder to quote both.

A question from buyers in Panama

Can I import from China without a corredor de aduanas? The 2008 customs decree requires a corredor for imports, with exceptions such as personal and household goods of individuals up to B/.4,000 CIF. An order for resale does not fall under that exception, so confirm with your corredor. SourciaVera does not act as a corredor.
