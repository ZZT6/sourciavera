What is different when you import to Chile

Words that change from country to country

In Chile, llantas are the wheels and tires are neumáticos, the opposite of Mexico, where llantas are tires. A license plate is a patente and the trunk is the maleta. This mix-up is a classic cause of wrong orders, so never rely on the Spanish word alone.

Time zone and how we communicate

Chile changes its clocks twice a year: it is on UTC−4 for part of the year and UTC−3 in summer time, so Guangzhou (UTC+8) is 12 or 11 hours ahead. A visit that starts at 9:00 in Guangzhou starts at 21:00 or 22:00 the evening before in Santiago. You get the report in Spanish or English, and we can stream the visit to you live on WhatsApp while it happens.

About importing from China

Importing from China into Chile: the agreement, customs and what Chile buys

A free trade agreement with China, in force since 2006

Chile and China have had a free trade agreement in force since 1 October 2006. It is listed among Chile's agreements in force on the SUBREI website, and the customs service (Servicio Nacional de Aduanas, SNA) announced its entry into force in Oficio Circular 467. This matters for your landed cost. Chile's general import duty is 6% of the CIF value (cost, insurance and freight, the price at the Chilean port; our guide to FOB, EXW, CIF and DDP quotes explains the terms), and the SNA says the duty can be exempt or reduced for goods from a country with which Chile has an agreement (SNA: import taxes). Whether your product qualifies depends on its tariff line and on proof of origin: the SNA lists a certificate of origin among the documents needed to claim a preference (SNA: how to import).

IVA, the value-added tax, is 19% and is calculated on the CIF value plus the duty. The SNA's own example: CIF value US$1,000, duty US$60, IVA US$201.40, total US$261.40 at the full 6%. Ask your agente de aduanas to confirm the duty for your tariff line before you pay a deposit, and ask your supplier early who will issue the origin document: a trading company can only pass on what the factory gives it.

Customs in Chile: the SNA and your agente de aduanas

Commercial goods up to US$1,000 invoiced can be cleared by the importer in a simplified procedure. Above US$1,000 FOB you must hire an agente de aduanas, who prepares the Declaración de Ingreso (DIN) from your documents. Every commercial import needs the original bill of lading, the original commercial invoice and the importer's sworn declaration on the price, and a packing list is required for containerized goods (SNA). Ask the supplier to check that invoice, packing list and bill of lading match before the cargo sails.

A question from buyers in Chile

Do I pay the 6% duty on goods from China? Not necessarily. The SNA says goods from a country with a trade agreement can enter exempt or at a reduced duty, and Chile has had one with China since 2006. Whether your product qualifies depends on its tariff line and on proof of origin, so ask your agente de aduanas to check both before you order. We are not a customs broker.
