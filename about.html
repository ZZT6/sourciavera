Who We Are & Why We Exist

A factory verification agency built for buyers that most of China trade never bothered to serve.

Our Story

In 2025, I made my first trip into the Mexican market — and it changed how I saw trade with China.

What struck me wasn't the opportunity. It was the people: hardworking importers across Latin America, consistently priced out of fair deals — not for lack of a strong business, but because they spoke Spanish, and most Chinese factories only speak Mandarin.

I watched good buyers get quoted worse prices, get ignored by factories, or get stuck working through agents who didn't understand their market — simply because of a language gap that had nothing to do with the quality of their business.

That's the gap SourciaVera exists to close.

Our Mission

We believe a language barrier should never be the reason a hardworking importer in Mexico, Guatemala, or Colombia can't access the same verified factories, the same fair pricing, and the same level of trust that English-speaking buyers take for granted.

Our Values
Transparency first.

We verify factories on the ground — not just on paper — before we ever recommend them to a client.

Bilingual by design, not as an afterthought.

Every quote, every report, every conversation happens in the language our client is most comfortable in.

Fair deals, both ways.

We believe good factories and good buyers both deserve honest partners in the middle — not middlemen who profit from confusion.

We're still early.

But we believe that with the right people bridging the right gap, Chinese manufacturing and Latin American business don't have to be worlds apart — they can be genuine partners.
