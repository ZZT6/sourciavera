About Us

You've been there.
We fix that.

SourciaVera is a bilingual team based in Guangzhou — built to handle every step between your order and your doorstep.

Sound familiar?

Before we tell you who we are, let us tell you what we've seen happen to importers who tried to go it alone:

Deposit paid. Factory stops responding. Money gone.
Samples arrived perfect. Bulk order looked nothing like them.
Goods held at customs. Nobody on the China side can explain why.
Supplier quoted you double what they quoted someone who speaks Mandarin.

These aren't edge cases. They happen every week to buyers who are otherwise running solid businesses. The common thread: no one on the ground in China who was actually on their side.

That's the only problem SourciaVera exists to solve. We handle China — so you can focus on your business.

Our Mission

We believe a language barrier should never be the reason a hardworking importer in the US, UK, Mexico, Guatemala, or Colombia can't access the same verified factories, the same fair pricing, and the same level of protection that the biggest players take for granted. Hand us your requirements — we handle the rest.

Our Team

SourciaVera was founded by Zhang Peng, who leads verification strategy and client relationships directly — every engagement still goes through him, not a rotating account manager. Execution isn't one person wearing every hat, though: a specialist team handles the ground, product, and logistics work below.

Ground Team

Verification & Audit

On-site coordinators across Guangdong and East China. They visit factories, film walkthroughs, run audits, and supervise production. Same-day reach to any supplier in the Pearl River Delta.

Category Team

Category Specialists

Every question goes to someone who actually knows the product — auto parts, chemicals, construction, LED — not a generalist reading from a script. You get real technical answers, not the runaround you'd get from an Alibaba rep who barely knows their own catalog.

Logistics Team

Shipment & Documents

Freight coordination, pre-shipment inspection, document review, and customs prep. We don't hand you off to a freight forwarder and disappear — we stay through to dispatch.

What We Stand For
Certainty over optimism.

We verify on the ground — not on paper. We report what we actually find, even when it's not what you hoped to hear.

Your side, not the factory's.

Tell us what you need — bluntly, directly, no sugar-coating. We welcome every suggestion, even the harsh ones. Criticism makes us better.

Real friends, not one-time transactions.

We're not chasing a single order — we want clients for 5, 10 years or more, real friendships built over time. One day, when our team visits your city, we want a true friend already waiting there: someone who'll show us around, share a meal, and make us feel at home.

Ongoing, not one-and-done.

We don't walk away after the verification visit. Supervision, production follow-up, and QC are part of how we work — because a single audit doesn't protect a 60-day production run.
