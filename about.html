About Us

You've been there.
We fix that.

Your procurement team in China, from the first supplier check to shipment. You don't need to understand China's supply chain. That's what we're here for.

Sound familiar?

Before we tell you who we are, let us tell you what we've seen happen to importers who tried to go it alone:

Deposit paid. Factory stops responding. Money gone.
Samples arrived perfect. Bulk order looked nothing like them.
Goods held at customs. Nobody on the China side can explain why.
Supplier quoted you double what they quoted someone who speaks Mandarin.

These aren't edge cases. They happen every week to buyers who are otherwise running solid businesses. The common thread: no one on the ground in China who was actually on their side.

That's the only problem SourciaVera exists to solve. We handle China — so you can focus on your business.

Our Mission

We believe a language barrier should never be the reason a hardworking importer in the US or UK can't access the same verified factories, the same fair pricing, and the same level of protection that the biggest players take for granted. Hand us your requirements — we handle the rest.

Our Team

Founded in 2025, SourciaVera is an independent third-party verification company built around one job: putting real people on the ground in Guangzhou to investigate whether a supplier is actually who they claim to be — before you wire a deposit. The company was founded by Zhang Peng (Miguel Lopez), who still leads verification strategy and client relationships directly on every engagement, not a rotating account manager. Execution isn't one person wearing every hat, though: the team works in three groups: procurement, transport and logistics, and foreign trade. Factory visits, video walkthroughs and production follow-up are carried out by our team in Guangzhou.

What We Cover

We don't try to be everything to everyone. We focus on three verticals where we can actually tell a good factory from a good sales pitch:

Auto Parts

Aftermarket and OEM-compatible parts, LED lighting, NEV components, brake systems, sensors, and more.

Construction Materials

Container homes, steel structures, prefab buildings, roofing, doors & windows, and building hardware.

Badges, Medals & Keychains

Enamel pins, challenge coins, award medals, custom keychains, and other promotional metal goods.

Within those categories, engagements range from a single factory verification to full-scope support: pre-shipment inspection, factory audits, ongoing production supervision, sourcing and procurement on your behalf, and dispute resolution when a deal has already gone wrong.

What We Stand For
Certainty over optimism.

We verify on the ground — not on paper. We report what we actually find, even when it's not what you hoped to hear.

Your side, not the factory's.

Tell us what you need — bluntly, directly, no sugar-coating. We welcome every suggestion, even the harsh ones. Criticism makes us better.

Real friends, not one-time transactions.

We're not chasing a single order — we want clients for 5, 10 years or more, real friendships built over time. One day, when our team visits your city, we want a true friend already waiting there: someone who'll show us around, share a meal, and make us feel at home.

Ongoing, not one-and-done.

The verification itself is a single on-site visit. A single audit doesn't protect a 60-day production run, so for buyers who want us to stay with the order there are two ways: on-site supervision and pre-shipment inspection can be booked on their own by the man-day, at the rates listed under Our Services, or they are included when we run the order through our procurement service.

Most Clients

We start every relationship the same way — by listening to what you actually need, not running through a script. For most importers, that's still a one-time job: verify the factory, hand you the evidence, let you close the deal yourself. If you'd rather not negotiate the order directly, we can also do that for you — a separate, optional step, priced at 10% of the order value.

For the Right Clients

Some clients need a partner, not a one-time report. For buyers ready to grow into a long-term relationship, we go further — vetting and selecting suppliers on your behalf and standing behind ongoing quality control. We're not chasing a single order anymore — we become a partner in the business you're building.

Our own team in Guangzhou handles sourcing, factory verification, negotiation, production follow-up and warehousing (storage, consolidation, packing and quality checks). For every order we handle through this service, we inspect the goods at the factory before they leave China, on camera, and send you the video (every unit on small batches, agreed sampling on large orders). International freight, customs clearance and payments are handled through partners we coordinate.
