Real Case: Four “Manufacturers” on Alibaba Turned Out to Be One Trading Company

Aug 21, 2026 · 5 min read · Zhang Peng, Founder
Back to Blog

A client came to us with a shortlist of four Alibaba suppliers, all offering 50% hydrogen peroxide, all carrying the “Verified Supplier” badge. Before wiring a deposit, they wanted us to confirm what they were actually buying from.

The Shortlist Looked Solid

Four different company names, four different Alibaba storefronts, each claiming in-house manufacturing. On paper, nothing separated them — similar pricing, similar photos, similar production claims.

What the Business License Cross-Check Revealed

We ran each company's business license against China's National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System (NECIPS) and checked the registered business scope. None of the four had a manufacturing scope that covered hydrogen peroxide production — all four were registered as trading or import-export entities, not producers.

The Follow-Up Call That Confirmed It

We contacted each supplier directly and asked for a live production line video and specific batch numbers. Three couldn't produce real-time footage of an operating plant. One sent an old promotional video — closer inspection showed it belonged to a different company's marketing material entirely.

Tracing It Back to One Actual Producer

All four storefronts were reselling from the same handful of real manufacturers in the Shaoguan area. The actual producers don't sell direct to small overseas buyers, so a layer of resellers had built four separate-looking Alibaba profiles around the same supply.

What This Almost Cost the Client

Based on the badge and product photos alone, the client was preparing a 30% deposit to one of the four — with no way of knowing, from the platform alone, that it wasn't a factory at all.

What We Did Instead

We identified and negotiated a direct introduction to an actual manufacturer — a producer with roughly 270,000 tons/year of capacity — cutting out the reselling markup and adding real accountability the client could verify.

The Lesson

A “Verified Supplier” badge confirms a business exists and passed a basic check. It does not confirm the business manufactures the product itself. Two checks reliably separate a factory from a trading company: cross-referencing the registered business scope, and requesting live, unedited production video on request.
