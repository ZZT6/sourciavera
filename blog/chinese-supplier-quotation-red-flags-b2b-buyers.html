7 Chinese Supplier Quotation Red Flags B2B Buyers Must Catch Before Paying

Sourcing Risk
SourciaVera TeamNov 22, 2026 · 7 min read

Short answer: Freeze any deposit if a Chinese supplier quotation omits Incoterms 2020 delivery points, material tolerances, legal entity registration, or AQL inspection terms. Quotes priced 20% to 35% below comparable factories, demanding over 50% upfront, or listing unrelated bank accounts signal commercial risk.

Why Price Outliers Before Scope Lock Signal Hidden Exclusions

When a supplier quotes 20% to 35% below three competing factories on the same drawing, the reduction rarely comes from efficiency alone. In industrial equipment and auto parts orders, low headline numbers frequently strip out export crating, voltage conversions (such as 480V/60Hz motors), spare wear parts, or local port trucking.

A quote issued within 2 hours for a custom-engineered item—without asking for drawings, tolerances, voltage, or packaging weight—shows the seller is guessing rather than costing a real bill of materials. Always lock the technical specification sheet before comparing unit prices.

7 Quotation Red Flags to Check Before Wiring a 30% Deposit

Before signing a proforma invoice, audit every line against these seven measurable checkpoints:

  1. Missing or ambiguous Incoterms 2020 point: Writing only "FOB China" instead of "FOB Shenzhen" or "EXW Guangzhou" leaves inland trucking and export customs fees ($300 to $1,200+ per container) unassigned. Review our Incoterms definition and FOB vs EXW vs CIF vs DDP breakdown.
  2. No material grade or dimensional tolerance: Phrases such as "stainless steel" instead of "304 stainless steel, 1.5 mm ±0.1 mm" allow downgrade substitution during mass production.
  3. Front-loaded payment terms above 30%: Standard B2B manufacturing uses 30% deposit and 70% balance after inspection. Demands for 50% to 100% T/T upfront on standard goods remove buyer leverage.
  4. No pre-shipment inspection or AQL clause: Omitting Acceptable Quality Limit (AQL) acceptance criteria under ISO 2859-1 leaves defect disputes unresolved. On a 5,000-unit lot at General Level II, the standard sample is 200 units (verify exact numbers with our AQL calculator).
  5. Unverifiable or mismatched beneficiary entity: The quotation header lacks the 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) or lists a personal account or unrelated Hong Kong company.
  6. Missing packaging and carton dimensions: Without gross weight, net weight, and carton CBM, your freight forwarder cannot calculate container loading or ocean freight accurately.
  7. Silence on lead time, warranty, and spare parts: Vague timelines ("about 3–4 weeks") with no start trigger (deposit receipt vs. sample approval) and no written defect remedy shift delay risk entirely to the buyer.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is an unusually low Chinese factory quote a warning sign?

When one supplier quotes 20% to 35% below three comparable factories on identical specs, the price gap typically comes from thinner steel or plastic, omitted accessories, unquoted export packaging, or trading-company bait pricing that rises after your deposit is locked.

What must a Chinese supplier quotation include before wiring a 30% deposit?

Require the exact Chinese legal entity name, 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code, Incoterms 2020 delivery point, material grade and dimensional tolerances, packaging specs, HS code reference, lead time in calendar days, AQL inspection clause, and matching corporate bank details.

Should a Chinese supplier quote list a personal or unrelated Hong Kong bank account?

No. A mainland export factory normally receives USD into a corporate account under its registered legal name or a documented subsidiary. If a quotation lists a personal name or unrelated Hong Kong entity without ownership proof, verify the entity before sending funds.

How can I verify whether a Chinese factory quotation reflects real manufacturing capacity?

Start with a $49 remote check (48-hour report verifying GSXT registration, manufacturer vs. trader status, and payment-account match). For larger deposits, book an on-site factory verification from $449 or a live video factory check at $120 per hour.
