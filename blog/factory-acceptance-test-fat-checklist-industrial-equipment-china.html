Factory Acceptance Test (FAT) Checklist for Industrial Equipment in China

Industrial Machinery
SourciaVera TeamNov 26, 2026 · 8 min read

The short answer: A complete China FAT checklist locks five checkpoints before you pay the 70% balance: 480V/60Hz motor and cabinet verification, 0.01 mm mechanical tolerances, a 2-to-4-hour continuous load run, live E-stop safety interlock testing, and a dated punch list signed before crating.

Verified as of November 26, 2026.

Pre-FAT Setup: Nameplate, Voltage, and Raw Material Verification

Before powering up a single motor, verify that the physical machine built on the shop floor matches your Proforma Invoice and the supplier's 18-digit GSXT legal registration. Chinese plants operate natively on 380V 50Hz three-phase power, while North American and Panamanian buyers routinely require 480V 60Hz or 220V 60Hz setups.

Always photograph the riveted metal motor nameplates, PLC and VFD inverter model stickers, and main transformer taps inside the electrical enclosure. For critical pressure vessels or certified steel grades, distinguish field verification from accredited testing: on-site FAT confirms component brands, dimensions, and running output, while destructive alloy chemistry or ASME U-Stamp compliance requires CNAS, ISO 17025, or authorized code bodies.

The 6-Stage Factory Acceptance Test (FAT) Checklist for China Machinery

Use our overview of what a Factory Acceptance Test covers for China equipment alongside this six-stage field checklist when inspecting industrial equipment in China:

  1. Electrical cabinet & voltage audit: Confirm 480V/60Hz (or contract voltage), phase sequence, grounding continuity, DIN-rail labeling, and matching breaker amperage using a calibrated multimeter and clamp meter.
  2. Mechanical tolerances & visual finish: Measure critical shaft bores, plate thicknesses, and frame alignment with a 0.01 mm digital caliper under 500+ lux lighting at 50 cm, checking welds for porosity and paint adhesion.
  3. 30-minute no-load warmup: Run motors, conveyors, and gearboxes unloaded for 30 minutes to verify rotation direction, abnormal bearing noise, and smooth belt or chain tracking.
  4. 2-to-4-hour continuous load run: Feed actual or simulated material for 2–4 hours, logging steady amp draw, hydraulic pressure gauges, throughput per hour, and bearing housing temperature stabilization.
  5. Safety interlocks & E-stop shutdown: Press every Emergency Stop button and open interlocked safety guards under live power to confirm immediate power cut and zero unintended restart.
  6. Punch list, spares & export crating: Log any defects on a numbered punch list, hold final payment until re-tested on camera, count spare wear parts, and verify bolted steel skids or fumigated ISPM-15 wooden crates for heavy units over 40 kg.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long should a Factory Acceptance Test load run take for Chinese machinery?

Plan for at least 2 to 4 hours of continuous operation under real or simulated production load after a 30-minute no-load warmup. Short 5-minute demo clips never reveal hydraulic oil leaks, motor overheating, or bearing vibration.

How do I verify 480V 60Hz electrical compatibility during a China FAT?

Inspect the physical metal motor nameplates, VFD inverter ratings, and transformer wiring inside the control cabinet on unedited video—since Chinese plants run 380V 50Hz natively and sometimes quote export voltage without rewiring.

What should happen if the machine fails an item on the FAT checklist?

Record every deficiency in a dated punch list with target fix dates and hold the 70% balance payment until a re-test confirms repairs on camera. Never allow crating or container loading before punch-list sign-off.

Does an on-site FAT replace ASME or CNAS laboratory certification?

No. An on-site FAT checks assembly quality, electrical interlocks, dimensional tolerances, and running performance, whereas pressure-vessel stamps or destructive alloy metallurgy require accredited CNAS, ISO 17025, or ASME inspection bodies.
