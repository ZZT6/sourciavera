How do I buy a single product from the Alibaba website for my personal use?

Import Guide
SourciaVera TeamAug 26, 2026 · Updated Oct 24, 2026 · 8 min read

The short answer: sometimes you can, but not on every listing. Alibaba.com is built for business buyers, so each supplier sets the smallest quantity it sells. Look for a listing whose price tiers start at one piece, or ask for a single unit as a sample. Expect a higher unit price and costly shipping, and pay inside Alibaba.com so Trade Assurance can apply.

You may have found exactly what you wanted on Alibaba at a price that looks too good, then seen "Min. order: 100 pieces" and wondered whether you are even allowed to buy. You are not doing anything wrong: the page is written for businesses buying in bulk, so the buttons and the prices assume that.

Can an Individual Buy One Item on Alibaba.com?

Alibaba Group describes Alibaba.com as a business-to-business platform and AliExpress as a global retail platform for consumers (Alibaba.com and AliExpress on Alibaba Group's own site, read Oct 8, 2026). Alibaba's seller guide defines the MOQ as the lowest number of units a wholesale supplier allows buyers to purchase at one time, and every supplier sets its own (Alibaba seller guide, Nov 19, 2020). So it depends on the supplier and the listing: some will sell you one unit, some will not.

How Do You Find a Supplier Who Will Sell One Unit?

  1. Read the price tiers on the listing. Look for a row that starts at 1 piece. If the first row starts at 100, the listing is priced for bulk.
  2. Check the unit before you compare prices. A price per set is not a price per piece; see the difference between "set" and "piece".
  3. Look at items that are already in stock. Alibaba's seller guide says products in its Ready to Ship channel can have MOQs as low as 5 or 10 units. That is close to one, but not always one.
  4. If the MOQ is higher than one, message the supplier and ask whether they sell a single unit or a sample. Alibaba's guide to requesting samples says the buyer typically pays for the sample and its shipping, and that you can ask for that cost to be taken off a later full order (Alibaba, Feb 3, 2021). For personal use there is no later order, so plan to pay the whole amount.
  5. Get the price in writing, with shipping to your address, before you pay.

What Will One Item Really Cost?

Add three things: the item at its small-quantity price, shipping to your door, and any duties and taxes your country charges. The shipping part is the one that surprises people. Alibaba's own guide to requesting samples says to expect around $50–$100 for a sample plus express air shipment on a product that retails for $20–$50, because sending a small package a long distance is relatively expensive. Here is the shape of it with made-up numbers:

These numbers are illustrative, not real quotes. The point is the pattern: on one item, shipping can be bigger than the item. Duties and taxes depend on your country and the value of the shipment, and the rules change, so check your customs authority instead of relying on a blog.

Is It Safe to Pay for One Item on Alibaba.com?

Alibaba says Trade Assurance applies when you pay through Alibaba.com's secure payment platform. It covers a product-quality or ship-date difference from what was agreed on the order, you can request a refund within 30 days after delivery, and your online order and communication records are the evidence (Alibaba's Trade Assurance page for buyers, read Oct 8, 2026). Two practical consequences: put the specifications and the price into the order, and keep the conversation on the platform. Paying a supplier by bank transfer or through a chat app takes you outside that protection.

In our guide Is Alibaba Trustworthy? I wrote that for small orders under $2,000 the math often works: use Trade Assurance, stay on-platform, document specs carefully and accept some quality risk. A single personal item is far below that line.

What stays hard to see from abroad is who actually makes the product. In one case we checked for a client, four suppliers with Verified Supplier badges that presented themselves as producers turned out to be trading or import-export companies. That is the gap a supplier check covers, and it is aimed at business orders, not at one item for yourself.

Is There an Easier Way to Buy One Item?

Often there is. AliExpress is Alibaba Group's global retail platform for consumers, and a local shop or marketplace may deliver faster and make returns simpler. Compare the landed price, with shipping, duties and taxes, before you decide. We looked at that platform's minimums in What would be the minimum amount of product that should be purchased from AliExpress?

What Should You Do Next?

  1. Decide what the item is for. For personal use, compare AliExpress and local options first. If it is a test for a business order, read the last step below.
  2. If you go ahead on Alibaba.com, look for listings with a low MOQ and read the price tiers and the unit.
  3. Message two or three suppliers and ask for one unit, with the price including shipping to your address. What the conversation looks like has a message you can copy.
  4. Compare the total landed price of each option, including duties and taxes.
  5. Pay only through Alibaba.com, with the specification written into the order, and check the goods as soon as they arrive: the refund request window is 30 days from delivery.
  6. If this item is a test before a larger business order, read Sample Looks Great, Bulk Order Is Garbage and 10 Things to Check Before Paying a Chinese Supplier Deposit before you place it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can an individual buy from Alibaba.com?

Alibaba Group describes Alibaba.com as a business-to-business platform, but whether you can buy one unit depends on each supplier. Some sell single units, often as samples; others require a minimum order.

Is Alibaba cheaper than AliExpress for one item?

Not necessarily. A single unit often costs more per piece than the bulk price, and shipping can be as large as the item itself. Compare the total landed price, including shipping, duties and taxes.

Does Trade Assurance cover a single item?

Alibaba says Trade Assurance protects orders paid through Alibaba.com's secure payment platform when product quality or the ship date differs from what was agreed on the order, and that you can request a refund within 30 days after delivery. Check the terms shown on your own order.

How do I ask a supplier for one unit?

Say what you want, ask for the price of one unit including shipping to your address, and ask the supplier to confirm the unit (piece or set) and the specification in writing.

Who pays for a sample on Alibaba.com?

Alibaba's guide to requesting samples says the buyer typically pays for the sample and its shipping, and that you can ask for that cost to be taken off the price of a later full order.

Summary and Next Step

Buying one item on a wholesale platform is possible with some suppliers, but it is not what the platform is designed for, and the total can be higher than a retail listing. Decide what you are buying it for, compare the landed price, and pay through the platform. You don't have to know everything about how wholesale sites work to try it once.

In short, one item can be ordered, but compare the landed price and pay through the platform. If you later plan a larger order for a business, the realistic risk is money tied up with a supplier nobody has checked, so check the basics before you place it.
