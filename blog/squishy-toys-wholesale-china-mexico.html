Squishy Toys from China: Why Kids Love Them and How to Buy Wholesale for Mexico & LatAm

Buyer's Guide
SourciaVera TeamJun 27, 2026 · Updated Nov 1, 2026 · 7 min read

The short answer: squishy toys sell well in Mexico and leave margin if you buy them right. Since January 1, 2026, Chinese toys pay a 30% tariff plus 16% VAT and need a Spanish label that meets the NOM. Your profit depends on choosing the right model and factory.

Verified as of November 1, 2026 against El Financiero's December 29, 2025 report on the toy tariff and the Ministry of Economy's official pages for NOM-015-SCFI-2007 and NOM-252-SSA1-2011. We did not read the original decree in the DOF.

Squishy toys and stress toys are cheap, light and easy to rotate for stores, stationery shops and online sellers. This guide covers why kids love them, which models exist and what to watch if you want to buy squishy toys wholesale in China.

A scene that repeats in many homes

Picture a 9-year-old girl in Guadalajara with more than 60 squishies in her room: little animals, fruit, cakes, stars. Some fit in the palm of a hand; others look like giant desserts. Her younger brother is starting his own collection, and every trip to the supermarket or toy store is a chance to hunt for a new model. (Illustrative example, not a real customer.)

That is what drives the trend: it is not one toy, it is a collection. And a collection means repeat purchases.

Why stress toys are so popular

1. The soft texture feels good. Touching and squeezing something soft is comforting.

2. Repetitive motion helps people switch off. Squeezing and watching the squishy slowly regain its shape is simple, and for some people it helps focus on something physical for a moment.

3. It is a break from screens. Instead of swiping on glass, a child touches, squeezes and feels different textures.

4. A small tool for tense moments. Some kids use them when nervous, bored or trying to concentrate. They do not solve emotional problems, but they can help a person feel a little calmer.

And they are not only for kids: many adults keep one next to the computer or in a bag for work breaks. That widens the audience for resellers.

Which types of squishies sell best

  • Mini animals: small, cheap and easy to carry in a backpack. They work for single-piece sales or surprise bags.
  • Bread and cakes: they imitate food, feel very soft and are usually slow-rising foam.
  • Fruit and candy: bright colors that work well in box displays.
  • Silicone or TPR figures (mochi style): more durable and washable.

Note that many of the most sought-after squishies are licensed characters. Those cannot be made or imported without the brand owner's permission; at customs and on marketplaces that is a real risk. The safe route is generic designs or your own design.

What to know before importing squishies from China to Mexico

To import toys from China to Mexico in 2026, these five points change your cost and your risk:

Requirements for importing squishies into Mexico. Sources: El Financiero (Dec 29, 2025) and PLATIICA pages, Ministry of Economy. Checked November 1, 2026.
PointWhat to know
TariffSince January 1, 2026, toys from countries without a trade agreement with Mexico (China included) pay a 30% tariff plus 16% VAT. Run your numbers with that included and confirm the exact tariff line (chapter 95, heading 9503) with your customs broker.
LabelingNOM-015-SCFI-2007 (commercial information, toy labeling): a Spanish label with recommended age, warnings, country of origin and importer details. It is far cheaper for the factory to print it during production than to label in Mexico.
Heavy metalsNOM-252-SSA1-2011: bioavailability limits for heavy metals in toys and school supplies. Hand-painted squishies or ones with glossy finishes need the most checking.
OdorCheap foam squishies can have a strong chemical smell. In 2018 Denmark's environmental protection agency tested 12 squishies and reported emissions of substances such as dimethylformamide, styrene and toluene; the European toy industry disputed the study. A squishy that smells strongly when the box opens is a signal to change supplier or request a lab test.
Small partsMini models are not for children under 3. The warning must be on the label.

Seasons. In Mexico the peaks are Children's Day (April 30), back to school, and Christmas and Three Kings' Day. Between production and ocean freight, plan to order two to three months ahead.

Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum order for squishy toys from China?

It depends on the factory and the model. Catalog models usually allow lower quantities; a custom design needs a mold and a higher minimum. If you are just starting, write to us: we also handle small batches.

How much tariff do Chinese toys pay in Mexico in 2026?

30% tariff plus 16% VAT for countries without a trade agreement with Mexico, such as China, according to the business press we checked. Confirm the exact tariff line with your customs broker.

Which NOM standards do squishies need to be sold in Mexico?

Mainly NOM-015-SCFI-2007 (toy labeling) and NOM-252-SSA1-2011 (heavy-metal limits in toys and school supplies).

Can I order squishies with my own design or logo?

Yes. It is common for private brands, promotional gifts and events. A new mold is made and a sample is approved before production.

Can I import squishies of famous characters?

Only with a license from the brand owner. Without one, you risk customs holding the goods or marketplaces taking your listings down.

Conclusion

Squishies are not just a fad: in an increasingly digital world, something small, soft and fun in the hands still has its place, and kids collect them. For resellers, that means repeat purchases. The difference between winning and losing is the right model, a real factory, an odor-free product and a NOM-compliant label, with the numbers already run at the 2026 tariff.
