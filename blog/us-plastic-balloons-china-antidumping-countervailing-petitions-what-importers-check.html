US AD/CVD Petitions on Chinese Plastic Balloons

Market News
SourciaVera TeamNov 21, 2026 · 6 min read

The short answer: On November 18, 2026, US producers filed antidumping and countervailing duty (AD/CVD) petitions on certain Chinese plastic balloons, alleging 50% to 276% dumping margins. No duties are active yet, though preliminary cash deposits could begin in February 2027 (CVD) and April 2027 (AD).

Verified as of November 21, 2026 against the trade-law summary published by Clark Hill (November 19, 2026) and trade reporting in MLex (November 19, 2026). We did not open the full petition filing or see a Chinese Ministry of Commerce statement in today's feed.

This briefing summarizes the proposed product scope, estimated preliminary deposit dates, and supplier checks for US seasonal, party-supply, and promotional buyers sourcing from China. Your licensed US customs broker determines HTS classification and cash deposit exposure on your entries.

What the November 18 Petitions Allege and Expected Timetable

According to Clark Hill, US producers submitted AD and CVD petitions to the Department of Commerce ("Commerce") and the U.S. International Trade Commission ("USITC") on November 18, 2026. The petitions allege dumping margins of 50% to 276% and countervailable subsidies above the de minimis threshold. Those percentages are petitioner allegations; Commerce has not calculated any official rate or deposit requirement yet.

Which Plastic Balloons Are Covered vs. Excluded?

According to Clark Hill, the proposed merchandise scope focuses on consumer and decorative laminated film balloons:

  • Included construction: Plastic balloons made from two or more pieces of extruded, laminated, and/or coated plastic film adhered at the edges except for the inflation opening, in plain, colored, or printed finishes—typically used for parties, celebrations, and retail decor.
  • Size-based exclusions: The scope size limits exclude smaller plastic balloons used in medical applications and larger plastic balloons used in weather or scientific applications.

As seen in other recent US trade-remedy petitions—such as the Chinese wooden fence components AD/CVD investigations and Chinese van trailer duties—final scope wording can adjust slightly when Commerce publishes its initiation notice.

What Is Still Unknown

  • Formal Commerce initiation and HTS list: Commerce must review sufficiency before issuing an initiation notice in the Federal Register.
  • Official preliminary rates: Whether actual calculated CVD/AD rates resemble the 50%–276% petition claims will depend on respondent participation and Commerce calculations.
  • Chinese official response: Today's news feed did not include an official statement from China's Ministry of Commerce.

What US Importers Should Check With Chinese Suppliers Now

Whether you work with a China sourcing agent or buy directly from a manufacturer in Zhejiang or Guangdong, verify four items before locking Q1 2027 orders:

  • Verify exact material construction: Confirm whether your SKUs use two-piece laminated/coated plastic film or single-piece latex/rubber, and review specs with your customs broker.
  • Check factory vs. trading company status: If Commerce initiates, only true manufacturers can file for separate rates; confirm via on-site factory supervision who actually manufactures your goods (just as importers do when sourcing aftermarket auto parts or seasonal consumer lines).
  • Lock deposit responsibility in writing: State on your Proforma Invoice who bears any AD/CVD cash deposits if shipments enter after February or April 2027.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are US anti-dumping or countervailing duties active on Chinese plastic balloons right now?

No. On November 18, 2026, US producers filed AD and CVD petitions with Commerce and the USITC. Neither agency has calculated duty rates or cash deposit requirements yet; preliminary cash deposits could begin as early as February 2027 for CVD and April 2027 for AD if the investigations proceed.

What dumping margins do the US petitioners allege against Chinese balloons?

According to Clark Hill's November 19, 2026 summary of the petitions, US producers allege dumping margins ranging from 50% to 276%, alongside countervailable subsidies above the de minimis level. These are petition allegations, not official Commerce findings.

Which plastic balloons are covered by the petition scope?

The proposed scope covers plastic balloons constructed from two or more pieces of extruded, laminated, or coated plastic film adhered at the edges except for the inflation opening, typically sold for celebrations and decor. Smaller medical balloons and larger weather or scientific balloons are excluded by size.

What should US party-supply importers verify with Chinese manufacturers now?

Confirm with your customs broker whether your SKUs match the laminated film balloon description, ask your Chinese supplier whether it plans to submit Commerce questionnaire responses if initiated, and check origin documentation before placing Q1 2027 seasonal orders.

Summary and Next Step

The November 18, 2026 US AD/CVD petitions put laminated plastic and foil party balloons from China on watch for potential cash deposits starting February–April 2027. Audit your SKU materials and verify your manufacturer's real identity before Q1 seasonal shipments sail.
