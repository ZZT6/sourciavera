How We Compared the Eight Companies

We looked at five facts a Latin American buyer can check on the public pages:

  1. Spanish: whether the site or the service is in Spanish.
  2. Factory visit: whether the company itself says it goes to the plant.
  3. Published price: whether it shows figures and what they include.
  4. Video: whether it says it delivers video of the visit.
  5. How it charges: a day rate, a fee per service or a commission.

How Each Company Charges

Tetra Inspection and SSTI International charge a flat rate per day. SSTI International states that it takes no commission on goods and none from factories. SourciaVera charges fixed fees for verification and inspection and, if it also buys for you, 10% of the order only when the purchase closes; it accepts no factory commissions (see the impartiality statement). Adrien China charges a flat fee for inspections and a commission for purchasing management. For the other companies we did not find how they charge.

What to Check Before You Choose

  1. Who pays the company. Ask in writing whether it receives a commission from the factory.
  2. What the rate includes. Travel, the report, the report language and the number of visits.
  3. What you receive after the visit. Ask for photos and unedited video, not only a summary.
  4. Whether staff go in person. Ask for the date, the time and continuous evidence from the entrance to the production line.

If you need follow-up during production, see our page on factory supervision in China. If you would rather have someone find the factory and buy for you, there is the China sourcing agent page. And if you only want to confirm a factory you already found, factory verification.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which companies supervise factories in China in Spanish?

According to their public pages on October 29, 2026, eight companies offer a Spanish site or Spanish service: SourciaVera, SSTI International, HQTS, QIMA, Tetra Inspection, PJCS International, Adrien China and China Box. They differ in whether they publish prices, visit the factory and deliver video; the table in the article gives the detail.

How much does an inspector cost per day in China?

Among the published prices: SSTI USD 130 per man-day in mainland China, with travel, photographs and an English report included; Tetra USD 240 in China; SourciaVera $399 for pre-shipment inspection and $499 for an audit. Adrien China publishes USD 397 per visit. The other companies do not publish prices.

Who delivers video of the factory visit?

According to their pages: SourciaVera delivers unedited video of every verification and Adrien China includes photos and video in its reports. Tetra offers remote inspection with live video and recorded evidence; SSTI mentions photographs. For the others we found no published information about video.

What is a man-day?

It is one working day of an inspector at the factory. Some companies charge it with travel and the report included, such as SSTI and Tetra according to their pages, and others charge per visit or per service. Before comparing prices, confirm what each rate includes.

How do I know whether the supervisor takes a commission from the factory?

Ask in writing who pays them. SSTI states on its page that it takes no commission on goods and none from factories; SourciaVera accepts no factory commissions. For the others we did not find that statement published, so it is worth asking.
