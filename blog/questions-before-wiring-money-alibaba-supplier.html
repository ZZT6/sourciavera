7 Questions to Ask Before You Wire Money to an Alibaba Supplier

Sep 4, 2026 · 7 min read · Zhang Peng
Back to Blog

The moment you're ready to pay a deposit is exactly when due diligence usually stops — right when it matters most. These 7 questions take five minutes to ask and separate suppliers who answer directly from ones who dodge. Next to each one: what you can judge yourself from their answer, and where an in-person check actually changes the picture.

1. "Can I see your business license number?"

Ask directly, in writing, and note whether they answer in the same conversation or go quiet for hours.

You Judge

Whether they hand it over immediately or start explaining why they "usually don't share that."

What We Add

We run the number against the government registry and confirm the manufacturing scope during our visit.

2. "What's your monthly production capacity for this exact product?"

Compare their answer against your order size. If your order alone exceeds their stated monthly capacity, something doesn't add up.

You Judge

Whether the number is oddly round, or specific enough to sound like it comes from a real production schedule.

What We Add

We count production lines and workers on the floor to confirm the stated capacity is physically possible.

3. "Who else has ordered this exact product from you in the last 6 months?"

Ask for a reference you can actually contact — not a testimonial screenshot that could belong to anyone.

You Judge

Whether they offer a name at all, or pivot to "we have a great reputation" without specifics.

What We Add

We can request and inspect recent shipping records on-site, which most buyers can't get access to remotely.

4. "Can we do a live video walkthrough this week?"

Propose a specific day and time. Vague "sure, anytime" answers followed by repeated delays are a signal on their own.

You Judge

How many times the scheduled call gets pushed back, and for what reason.

What We Add

We show up in person, which no amount of video call preparation can fully substitute for.

5. "What happens if the sealed sample doesn't match the bulk shipment?"

Get their answer in writing before you pay — not after a mismatch has already shipped.

You Judge

Whether they offer a concrete remedy (rework, discount, reshipment) or just reassurance.

What We Add

We inspect the bulk production against the sealed sample directly, before it ships — not after.

6. "Is this price negotiable, and why or why not?"

A supplier who can explain their cost structure — materials, labor, MOQ tiers — is more credible than one who just discounts on request.

You Judge

This one's on you — no site visit changes what a real cost explanation sounds like versus a blanket "I'll give you a special price."

What We Add

Not applicable. We'd rather tell you where a visit doesn't add value than sell you one anyway.

7. "Can we put these terms in a written contract on the platform?"

Insist on Alibaba's on-platform order confirmation — not just an agreement reached in chat messages.

You Judge

Whether they resist formalizing terms that they already agreed to verbally.

What We Add

We review contract terms in Chinese and English and flag ambiguous clauses before you sign.

What to Do With the Answers

One dodged question is worth a follow-up. Two or more, and the pattern is the answer — a supplier with nothing to hide doesn't need five extra messages to confirm a business license number. The goal isn't to interrogate every supplier on every order; it's to spend five minutes on the ones getting real money before you spend the money.
