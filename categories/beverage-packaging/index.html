Aseptic Tetra Pak Cartons (Water, Coconut Water, Juice, Milk)

Buyers frequently send supermarket photos of aseptic cartons and ask to fill them by hand through the top cap. After checking with three Chinese factories directly: aseptic cartons require dedicated automatic filling lines and cannot be manually filled under any circumstances.

Gable-Top Milk Cartons Still Require Top-Sealing Machinery

Stepping down from aseptic Tetra Pak to pure PE gable-top cartons does not enable hand filling. You still need at least a semi-automatic top heat-sealer at your plant; pre-sealing top and bottom at the factory to pour through a tiny spout does not work physically.

Manual viable
100% Manual Solution
Screw-Cap PET / HDPE Bottles Work Without Filling Machines

If your plant fills 100% manually, abandon the milk-carton shape and use cylindrical screw-cap bottles or wide-mouth containers: pour the water or juice first, then twist the cap shut.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions on custom beverage packaging from China

Can I fill Tetra Pak cartons manually if the factory pre-seals the top and bottom?

No. Aseptic Tetra Pak cartons require automatic filling machinery and cannot be filled by hand. Anyone can verify this directly with Chinese packaging factories; manual filling through a spout is not viable.

Do cheaper gable-top milk cartons work without filling machines?

No. Even lower-cost pure PE gable-top milk cartons still require at least a semi-automatic top-sealing machine on site. Attempting pure manual filling through a small screw cap on pre-sealed cartons fails physically.

What beverage packaging works if my operation is 100% manual?

Abandon the milk-carton shape and use screw-cap plastic bottles or wide-mouth containers. Workers fill the water, juice, or beverage first and twist the cap on afterward.

Why are 10,000-piece custom beverage carton quotes so much higher than $0.05 per unit?

Unit prices of $0.03 to $0.07 apply at 100,000+ units per size. On 10,000-piece trial orders, setup waste and printing plates raise unit quotes to roughly $0.40 to $0.74 USD.
